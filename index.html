Set up in an afternoon

No electrician and no drilling through walls, unless you want a permanent mount.

  1. Place

    Choose spots that cover your entries: front door, back yard, garage, side gate. Charge or plug in each device.

  2. Pair

    Open the Arlo app, scan the code on each device and follow the prompts to connect it to your Wi-Fi or hub.

  3. Customize

    Set motion zones, schedules and who gets notified. Tune alerts until they only show what matters to you.
